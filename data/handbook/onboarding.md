# Engineering Onboarding

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This guide covers what to expect in your first weeks.

## First week

On day one you receive your laptop and set up SSO and your hardware security key. Every new engineer is paired with an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 90 days. Aim to merge your first pull request by the end of your first week; your buddy will help you pick a small starter task.

## Required training

Complete security awareness training within your first 14 days. Engineers who will join the on-call rotation also complete incident response training before their first shadow shift.

## 30-60-90 day plan

Your manager shares a 30-60-90 day plan in your first week. By day 30 you should be shipping small changes independently; by day 60 you should own a small feature end to end; by day 90 you should be ready to join the on-call rotation.
