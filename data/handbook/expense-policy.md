# Expense Policy

Spend company money as if it were your own, and keep receipts.

## Approvals

Any expense above $500 needs written pre-approval from your manager before you spend it.

## Travel

Book flights in economy class for trips under 6 hours of flight time; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Hotels should be booked through the company travel tool.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase. Receipts are required for every item over $25.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, conference tickets, certifications and books. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

## Home office

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of $750 for a desk, chair, monitor or other equipment.
