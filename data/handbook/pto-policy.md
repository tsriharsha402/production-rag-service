# Time Off Policy

Rest is part of doing good work. This policy covers vacation, sick leave and company-wide days off.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, at about 2.08 days per month. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year; carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in the HR portal. Absences of more than 3 consecutive working days should be requested at least two weeks in advance and approved by your manager. Shorter absences only need a heads-up to your team.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO: employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is only needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Wellness days

In addition to PTO and public holidays, the whole company takes 4 wellness days per year, announced at the start of each year.
